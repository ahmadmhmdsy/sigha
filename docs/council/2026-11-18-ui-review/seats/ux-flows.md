## 3. Dimension judgement — 0.2.1

| Dimension | 0.2.0 | 0.2.1 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visibility of status | 2 | **3** | The OCR gate now reports and reveals itself live, per-row states are localized and openable (`convert-done-ar.png`), and notes speak Arabic. Still no batch position in the pinned bar and no destination in the result row/summary. |
| User control and freedom | 2 | **2** | Cancel now exists on the OCR card and in the converting bar; but no retry, no per-row delete, no undo for a cleared selection, and four "clear" scopes remain. |
| Error prevention and recovery | 1 | **2** | Arabic pages are accepted and visibly validated; the OCR dead end is now an in-place install; folder fallback is typed/localized. But an input the UI marks invalid is still convertible (N1), target resets are silent, and failed rows are dead ends. |
| Recognition vs recall | 1 | **2** | The disabled Convert now explains itself, the OCR route is on the page, and counts use typed plurals; the queue is still an icon-only destination with four names and no notification presence. |
| Cognitive load | 2 | **2** | Install-in-place removes the Settings detour and the card is scannable; but the six-section single scroll still buries progress/results, and the hint now competes with the empty-targets card (N3). |

**Verdict: the P0 is fixed end to end — this is a real usability repair, not a cosmetic one.** The app now
answers "can I do OCR?" on the screen that previously lied. It still cannot answer "what happened to my 12
files, and where did they go?" without leaving Convert: the pinned bar has no position, the done row holds a
`displayPath` it does not render, and the summary speaks counts but no location. That missing batch-outcome
surface remains the dimension's single most important problem; N1 (validated input still allowed to run) is
the cheapest high-value follow-up, and N2/N3 are the fix pass's own new consistency debts.
