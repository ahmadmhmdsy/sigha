## 5. Fatal flaw in the obvious plan — Fasad i'tibar

The obvious plan — green gate, device pass, upload now, build the P1s as 0.2.2 during the 14 days — rests on a false premise: that the window measures the product instead of a build. It measures a build. Play vitals are aggregates over the cohort and the success rate is compiled from tester reports — both name one build (`store-listing.md:287–295`); the checklist and the screenshots must come from the upload build (`store-listing.md:247–251,309`); and composition moves the entry point of every conversion (target choice), i.e. exactly the rows the checklist certifies. A 0.2.2 landing inside the 14 consecutive days therefore voids the evidence the test exists to produce, and leaves no way to attribute a failure to the build that caused it.
Two honest exits only: land both P1s before the checklist and upload once, or freeze 0.2.1 and ship them after day 14. "Fix during the test" is the one option that cannot be evidenced.
