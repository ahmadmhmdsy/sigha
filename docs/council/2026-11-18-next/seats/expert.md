## 4. Fatal flaw in the obvious plan — Fasad i'tibar
The obvious plan finishes the design list, then reads the closed test's pass/fail as the product verdict. That inference is invalid: the cohort is selected for device coverage, not sampled (store-listing.md:256-265), the build has no telemetry or counters (PRODUCT.md:121-124), and the gate counts opted-in days, not use. A tester who never reopens satisfies the clock; a tester who finds the app plain may still stay installed. The test can discover broken tasks and false claims; it cannot decide desire. Rank design work by craft and deadline, and use testers only for what they can show.

## 5. Red line before the 12-tester test begins
- The hardware gate is real: checklist A–F recorded on at least one Arabic-RTL and one English phone, failures filed (device-checklist.md:99-103; store-listing.md:247-248).
- The test has an instrument: outcome and recommendation questions asked of every tester plus an exit interview; without it the 14 days produce task lists, not a verdict.
- The truth floor holds: no unshipped feature visible in the build or the listing (upscaling card removed or hidden; PRODUCT.md:133-139), so every tester sentence is about the product that ships.
- Distribution gate: B6/B7 closed before any APK reaches a tester (release-blockers.md:20-22) — legal, not design, but it starts the clock.
