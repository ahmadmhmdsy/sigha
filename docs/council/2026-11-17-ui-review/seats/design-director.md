# Design Director

**Method:** read-only source + resource review of `ui/` (20 files), `theme/`, `res/values*/` and the product docs. No app-source edits, no Gradle build, no device or emulator. Contrast values are computed from the colour tokens; layout arithmetic is derived from declared dp sizes, not measured on a screen. No device QA has ever run (`PRODUCT.md:6-7`, `docs/qa/device-checklist.md:1-7`), so "what the code should render" is the strongest claim this report makes.

## Verdict

Sigha's interface is systematically assembled but not authored. It is a clean Material 3 utility with an emerald accent: the emerald/sand/ink identity named in `Color.kt` never reaches the text or surface layer (Material 3's default purple-grey neutrals still paint every label), sand survives only as icon-circle fill and a status colour, and the only visible mark is the stock `SwapHoriz` glyph, reused five times. The composition buries the one required decision — with a batch selected, the target chooser scrolls below the fold while the bottom Convert button sits disabled and silent, on top of a stacked bottom bar that costs over a third of a small phone. For an Arabic-first product the Arabic layer is translation, not authorship: the OCR pack and the privacy-policy body render in English inside Arabic screens, and there is no Arabic-tuned type.

## Strengths

1. **Rule-based composition, very little ad-hoc styling.** No raw `.sp` values exist outside `theme/` and every text style comes from `MaterialTheme.typography` (verified by grep: 0 matches in `ui/` outside `Type.kt`). One 20 dp gutter is repeated across Workbench, History, Queue, Settings, About, Privacy, Model packs and Licenses; `SectionHeader`, `SighaCard`, `EmptyState`, `FormatIcon`, `FormatBadge`, `StatusPill` are reused instead of re-invented (`components/Common.kt:86-198`, `components/SighaCard.kt:14-28`, `components/FormatUi.kt:94-126`). This is a sound base; the problems below are of authorship, not of discipline.
2. **String-layer localisation is genuinely complete.** EN/AR parity is 165/165 (`strings.xml`), 31/31 (`strings_ui.xml`) and 22/22 (`strings_legal.xml`), verified key-by-key, including legal copy (`values-ar/strings_legal.xml:1-28`). All 16 failure kinds map to localised headlines while the English engine diagnostic is demoted to "Show technical details" (`FailureUi.kt:12-28`, `QueueScreen.kt:242-263`), and navigational arrows are AutoMirrored (`Common.kt:73`, `HistoryScreen.kt:207`, `QueueScreen.kt:220`). The Arabic text is careful MSA, not machine translation.
3. **Trust is designed into the flows, not only the slogan.** The OCR download requires an explicit confirmation that states the size and warns on metered data (`ModelPacksScreen.kt:114-153`, `isMeteredConnection` at 393-398); About names the single network request in plain words (`AboutScreen.kt:105-119`, `strings.xml:168-171`); licences and the privacy policy are bundled for offline reading (`LicensesScreen.kt:382-425`, `PrivacyScreen.kt:110-119`); there are no dark patterns, upsell, or "rate us" interruptions. The empty-state copy is honest about scope (`strings.xml:40-42`) and the tagline stays inside the claims discipline of `PRODUCT.md:131-138`.

## Persona red flags

**1) أُم أحمد — 42, Baghdad. Phone-as-office, Arabic only, 360×640 dp class phone, data budget.** Converts receipts, a DOCX for a job application and lecture PDFs for her children.
- A scanned PDF fails; the Arabic error tells her to install the pack from Settings → Model packs (`values-ar/strings_ui.xml:7`). She lands on a card that says "OCR: English + Arabic … Downloads once, then needs no network." in English (`model/PackCatalog.kt:32-34` → `ModelPacksScreen.kt:277-285`). The one moment the Arabic-first promise matters, it breaks.
- She selects five files for one target. The target chooser is below the fold (`WorkbenchScreen.kt:255-268`); the bottom button is grey (`WorkbenchScreen.kt:661-664`) and says nothing (`strings.xml:48` unused). She taps it; nothing happens.
- Convert bar + nav bar take ~37% of her screen (`WorkbenchScreen.kt:162-170`, `SighaNavHost.kt:50-88`) and, in daylight, card boundaries are ~1.02:1 (`SighaCard.kt:20-26`, `Theme.kt:25-26`) — she cannot see where "her files" end and "options" begin.
- Checking privacy, she gets "لا نجمع أي بيانات." above an English policy body (`PrivacyScreen.kt:110-119`, `assets/legal/privacy-policy.md:1`). The trust claim she came for is not legible to her.

**2) Omar — 29, Erbil. Privacy-conscious early adopter, English UI, dark mode, compares with Files by Google.** He installs because he saw "no upload".
- The "Offline" badge that would prove the promise at the moment files are staged is defined and never rendered (`strings.xml:7`); the promise itself is a 12 sp subtitle that can ellipsize (`Common.kt:57-64`). His trust cue is conditional on screen width.
- Dark-mode launch flashes a light window (#F7FAF8 only, `themes.xml:3-7`), then snaps dark (`MainActivity.kt:37,42-47`) — his first impression of a "trustworthy" app is an unfinished one.
- After converting, "where is my file?" has two answers — Queue (session, behind a bullet-list icon) and History (last 50) with two different row designs (`QueueScreen.kt:137-167,172-313`; `HistoryScreen.kt:104-139,160-280`). He compares this to Files by Google's single results surface and reads it as a design gap.
- Nothing on screen identifies the product beyond the word "Sigha": stock swap glyph, M3 default neutrals (`Theme.kt:15-34`), no wordmark. He concludes it is a side project, not a product he should trust with private files.

## Qiyas card(s)

### Qiyas 1 — "One bar, one decision"
- **Al-Far'**: Sigha's workbench must carry pick → target → convert in one viewport, with a single bottom action that names its outcome.
- **Al-Asl**: Material 3's contextual bottom-action pattern as shipped in Files by Google and Google Photos — entering a selection/task state yields the navigation bar to one action that echoes the selected object, rather than stacking two bars.
- **Al-'Illa**: A primary CTA and the decision it depends on must be co-present in the viewport. When chrome or content pushes the decision out of view while the CTA is disabled, the operate-mode loop stalls: the user sees the primary control but cannot act, and cannot see what would unlock it. This is a structural cause, not a styling preference.
- **Conditions**: one target per batch (holds: `WorkbenchViewModel.kt:166-187` starts one target for all files); reachable targets are few enough to show as a chip row above the CTA; navigation must remain reachable during the flow (back stack / top bar); the running state can move to the status surface (notification + Queue).

### Qiyas 2 — "Author the scheme, don't spot-fix the accent"
- **Al-Far'**: Sigha's quiet, trustworthy, Arabic-first identity on a Material 3 base.
- **Al-Asl**: Material 3 brand theming, which requires a custom ColorScheme to supply *all* roles (including `onSurface`/`onBackground`/`onSurfaceVariant`), as done by Arabic-first regional products (Careem, Anghami) that also use an authored Arabic type family rather than the system default; open Arabic faces such as Noto Naskh Arabic / IBM Plex Sans Arabic exist for a licensed, small subset.
- **Al-'Illa**: Identity is carried by the neutral/ink layer and a small number of repeatable marks, not by the primary button alone. If ~90% of the pixels remain framework defaults, the product reads as a template no matter how good the accent colour is; conversely, restricting the category palette to a tonal family keeps semantic cues without a rainbow that fights the brand.
- **Conditions**: stay M3-compliant (all roles, accessible pairs ≥4.5:1 for 12 sp text, ≥3:1 for icons); font subset must not blow the APK budget and must render Arabic diacritics correctly at 12-24 sp; regional imagery must avoid cliché; assets must stay original — no Filee trade dress or assets (condition 1, `docs/council/qiyas-council.md:55`) and the Sigha name needs trademark clearance before launch (`docs/council/qiyas-council.md:81`).

## Fatal flaw

**The interface has no author.** Everything that would make it Sigha — the sand and ink that name its palette, an Arabic wordmark, an Arabic-tuned type rhythm, an offline cue at the moment private files are staged — exists in `Color.kt`, `strings.xml` and the docs but never on screen; the pixels are Material 3 defaults with an emerald button. For a product positioned entirely on trust (`PRODUCT.md:3,15-22`; `docs/council/qiyas-council.md:74-75`), an interface that looks like an unbranded template spends the trust it needs: a user can swap the wordmark and nothing identifies the app, the promise is a 12 sp subtitle, and the Arabic-first claim visibly fails in the OCR and privacy screens. The hierarchy failure (buried target, dead CTA) then turns a trust problem into a task-completion problem.

## Questions the design team should answer

1. With the words removed from the first screen, what identifies this as Sigha? If the answer is a stock swap-arrows glyph, what are we asking a first-time user to remember?
2. Why does the app default image quality (85, `Converter.kt:9`) and DPI for the user, but refuses to default the target format — then disables Convert without saying why?
3. Is Settings really one of the three most important destinations in an app whose job is "convert this, then find it"? What would be gained by putting Queue/Results where Settings is?
4. Where is sand and ink supposed to appear, and under what lighting/device conditions was that decided? Was any of it verified on a physical phone (the QA gate has not run: `PRODUCT.md:6-7`)?
5. Who owns Arabic typography? System font + Latin tracking + no plural rules is not an Arabic-first type system — is that a deliberate size-budget decision, or an omission to schedule?
6. Why do the Model packs card and the privacy-policy body remain English inside Arabic screens while the store listing claims a full Arabic interface (`store-listing.md:65`)? Which gets fixed: the app or the claim?
7. The screenshot plan expects an "Options sheet" (`store-listing.md:167`) and "3 images selected, targets row visible" (`166`) — neither state exists as built. Which artefact is the source of truth for the intended design, and where is it written down?
