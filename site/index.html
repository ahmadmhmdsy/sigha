“Recognised” is not “supported”. Nine of the 48 formats — TIFF, RTF, TAR, BZ2, XZ, YAML, XML, MOBI and FB2 — have no converter at all. Sigha names them so it can tell you why nothing can be done, and the app never offers them as targets.

02 What it does

File conversion that runs inside the phone, not inside a browser tab

The engines ship inside the APK, so a conversion never depends on a connection or on someone else's server. These are the jobs Sigha actually finishes in 0.2.1 — and the limits of each one.

01

PDF conversion on the device

Export PDF pages as JPG or PNG with a page range, pull out the text layer, or turn images and plain text into a PDF. Scanned pages fall back to OCR once the language pack is installed. Sigha converts PDFs and to PDF; it does not edit, merge, split or compress them.

PDF→JPGPDF→PNGPDF→TXTIMG→PDFTXT→PDF

02

Image format conversion, including HEIC and AVIF

Convert between JPG, PNG and WebP, read HEIC on Android 9+ and AVIF on Android 12+, and handle BMP and GIF. Quality is yours to set from 20 to 100, EXIF orientation is applied before conversion, and any image can become an A4 PDF page.

HEIC→JPGPNG→WEBPAVIF→PNGIMG→PDF

03

Office documents to PDF and text

DOCX, ODT and PPTX become readable text; DOCX and XLSX become PDF; XLSX and ODS export to CSV. This is text and table extraction, not layout-preserving conversion: images, text boxes and exact formatting are dropped, and only the first worksheet is read.

DOCX→PDFXLSX→CSVPPTX→TXTODT→TXT

04

Audio and video conversion with FFmpeg

Swap between MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG and OPUS; turn video into MP4, WebM, MKV or GIF; strip the audio track out of a video; choose the bitrate when the codec takes one. Metadata and chapters are dropped, and an unsupported codec fails with an explanation instead of a stack trace.

MKV→MP4VIDEO→MP3WAV→FLACMP4→GIF

05

Data, subtitles, e-books and archives

CSV, TSV and JSON interchange; SRT to VTT and back; EPUB to text or HTML; ZIP to TAR.GZ and back; 7Z to ZIP. RAR is not supported, 7Z cannot be created, and encrypted archives fail with a clear message rather than a half-written file.

CSV→JSONSRT→VTTEPUB→TXT7Z→ZIP

06

Optional OCR for scanned PDFs and photos

A 28 MB pack adds optical character recognition for images and scanned PDFs in English and Arabic. Nothing is fetched until you tap the button: the size, the licence and a metered-connection warning come first, and the download is resumable and checked against a pinned SHA-256.

JPG→TXTPDF→TXT

07

An Arabic interface, not a translated menu

The whole app speaks Arabic as well as English: right-to-left layout, Arabic counts and plural forms, an Arabic privacy policy, and an OCR pack that reads Arabic. Convert results and notifications are written in the language the phone is set to.

08

Results that land in your gallery, files and music apps

Converted images go to Pictures/Sigha, documents to Download/Sigha and audio to Music/Sigha — or to a folder you grant once through the system picker. On Android 10+ no broad storage permission is requested, and the last 50 conversions stay in History.

05 How it works

Three steps to convert a file on your phone

  1. 01

    Pick the files

    Open the system file picker, or share a file into Sigha from another app. Images, PDFs, media and archives can sit in one batch.

    The app reads the file only when you convert it.

  2. 02

    Pick the target format

    Sigha offers only the formats every selected file can reach, so a target that cannot work is never shown. It remembers your last target for images, documents, audio and video.

    Options appear only where they exist: quality, bitrate, page range.

  3. 03

    Convert, then keep or share

    Conversion runs in a queue on the device. Results go to Pictures, Download or Music under a Sigha folder, and reopening or sharing is one tap.

    A failed file can be retried without picking it again.

06 Privacy and legal

No account, no upload, no analytics — and one download you control

Privacy here is architecture, not a promise. The conversion engines are compiled into the APK, so there is no server address to send a file to and no cloud SDK that could.

  • Your files stay on the phoneEvery conversion runs locally. Sigha has no upload path, no account system and no cloud storage integration.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKNothing is measured, profiled or sent off the device, including crash reports.
  • No broad storage permissionOn Android 10+ Sigha writes through MediaStore and a folder you grant with the system picker.
  • Read the actual policyIt is in the repository, in English and Arabic, and it is short enough to finish.

07 Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Sigha work without internet?

Yes. Every conversion runs on the device. The one exception is the optional OCR language pack: a 28 MB download you start yourself, after which OCR also works offline.

Is Sigha free?

Yes. There is no price, no subscription, no ads and no in-app purchase in 0.2.1, and the source code is public on GitHub.

Is Sigha on Google Play?

No. Version 0.2.1 ships as a side-load QA build from GitHub Releases. It is signed with the Android debug key and 21 legal placeholders still block a Play Store release, so you install it by allowing “Install unknown apps” for the app you open the APK from. Play Protect may warn about an unknown developer; that is expected for this build.

Which formats can Sigha convert?

39 of the 48 recognised formats can be used as an input and 25 can be produced as an output, across 11 engine families. Nine recognised formats have no converter yet — TIFF, RTF, TAR, BZ2, XZ, YAML, XML, MOBI and FB2 — and “recognised” means the app can name the format, not convert it.

Does Sigha support Arabic?

Yes, completely. The interface exists in Arabic and English, each laid out in its own direction, with Arabic counts and plural forms, an Arabic privacy policy, and an OCR language pack that reads Arabic.

What does Sigha send over the network?

Only the OCR language pack, and only when you tap Download. It carries no file, no usage data and no identifier. There is no analytics SDK, no ad SDK and no account system in the app.

Which Android versions does Sigha support?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer. HEIC input needs Android 9 or newer and AVIF input needs Android 12 or newer; the app explains the limit on older devices.

Where do converted files go?

Images are saved to Pictures/Sigha, documents to Download/Sigha and audio to Music/Sigha, or to a folder you grant once through the system picker. The last 50 conversions stay in History so you can reopen or share them.
